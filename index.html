Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

We want you to love every purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, we're here to help.

1. Return Window

You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange. Items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Intimate apparel and swimwear (for hygiene reasons).
  • Beauty products that have been opened or used.
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.
  • Gift cards.

3. How to Initiate a Return

  • Email support@luxeshopusa.online with your order number and reason for return.
  • We will email you a prepaid return shipping label within 1–2 business days.
  • Pack the item securely and drop it off at any US postal carrier.

4. Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect your return (usually within 3–5 business days), we will notify you via email. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Bank processing times may vary.

5. Exchanges

If you need a different size or color, we will ship your replacement item free of charge once your return is received, subject to availability.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no cost to you, including return shipping.

7. Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund after 10 business days, please first check your bank account and contact your credit card company. If the issue persists, contact us at support@luxeshopusa.online.

8. Sale Items

Items purchased on sale (excluding "Final Sale" items) are eligible for store credit only, not cash refunds.
